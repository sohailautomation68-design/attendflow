# attendflow user guide

This guide is for academy owners, teachers, students and guardians. It describes what each screen does, step by step.

## 1. Getting started (academy owner)

1. Open the home page and choose **Start your academy**.
2. Enter the academy name, your name, your email and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. The dashboard shows a **Setup** checklist. Work through it:
   - Add a teacher (**Teachers**).
   - Create a class (**New class**).
   - Add students one by one, or import a CSV (see section 4).
   - Add a weekly timetable slot on the class page.
   - Mark the first attendance.

## 2. Taking attendance (teacher or admin)

1. On the dashboard, choose **Take attendance** for a class, or **Mark now** for a day in **Needs attendance**.
2. Pick the subject if the class has subjects. Choose **Whole class** otherwise.
3. Check the date at the top. Past days can be filled in.
4. For each student tap **P** (present), **A** (absent), **L** (late) or **Lv** (excused leave). Tap the same button again to clear it.
5. Use **Mark all unmarked as Present** for the usual case, then change the exceptions.
6. Tap **Save attendance**. A draft is kept on the device, so if the connection drops, tap Save again.

Rules:
- Teachers can change a saved sheet for **48 hours**. After that only an admin can change it, and must give a reason.
- Every change is recorded in **Audit** (admin only).
- Attendance cannot be marked on a day that was cancelled.

## 3. Timetable, cancelled days and generating days (admin)

- On a class page, **Timetable** holds the weekly slots (for example Monday 09:00–10:30).
- **Cancelled or holiday days**: choose a date and a reason. The day is never counted as an absence. **Restore** brings it back.
- **Create attendance days from the timetable**: choose a date range. An open day is created for each slot, so **Needs attendance** shows what is still unmarked.

## 4. Students (admin)

- **Add student**: name, roll number and optional guardian phone.
- **CSV import**: download the template from the class page, fill it in, paste it into **Import students from CSV**, then **Check file**. The preview lists problems with line numbers. Only when there are no problems does **Import** appear. Nothing is saved until you confirm.
- **Move to…**: transfers a student to another class. Earlier attendance stays with the old class.
- **Remove**: removes a student from the active list. Their history is kept.
- Tap a student's name to open their **profile**: attendance percentage, alerts, class history and recent records.

## 5. Reports

- Open a class, then **Report**.
- Pick a range (**Today**, **Last 7 days**, **This month**, or your own dates) and optionally one subject.
- **Download CSV** opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
- Percentage = (Present + Late) ÷ counted days. Excused days are left out. Cancelled days and days outside a student's enrolment are not counted. A dash (—) means there is nothing to count yet.

## 6. Absence alerts and follow-up

- **Alerts** lists students flagged by these rules:
  - 3 absences in a row.
  - Attendance below 75% (after at least 4 counted days).
  - 5 absences in the last 30 days.
- An admin can **Assign follow-up** to a teacher with a due date and a note.
- The assignee (or an admin) can **Mark done** and choose the reason the family gave, such as sickness.
- **Resolve alert** closes the alert when it is handled.

## 7. Notifications

- **Notifications** lists new alerts, follow-ups assigned to you, and correction decisions. **Mark all as read** clears the unread count shown in the menu.

## 8. Corrections

- **Students and guardians**: on **My attendance**, open **Request a correction**, choose the date and the correct status, and explain why.
- **Admins**: **Corrections** lists pending requests. **Approve** writes the change through the normal save path, so it is audited with the reason. **Decline** leaves the record unchanged.

## 9. Student and guardian logins (admin)

- Open a student's profile and use **Student or guardian login**.
- A **student login** sees only their own attendance.
- A **guardian login** sees only the children linked to it. You must tick **Guardian consent recorded** first.
- Share the temporary password securely and ask the user to keep it private.

## 10. Plans and the platform owner

- Each academy has a plan (Starter, Standard, Professional) with student and teacher limits. Adding beyond the limit shows a message to upgrade.
- The platform owner sees a list of academies with counts only, and can change plan, status and enabled state. A reason is required and every change is audited.
- A suspended academy cannot sign in.
- Prices are experimental.

## 11. Staying safe

- Use a strong password and do not share accounts.
- Sign out on shared computers.
- Only record attendance for students you are responsible for.

## 12. Getting help

Use the contact form on the public website. Note that payment, password-reset email and messaging are not active in this version; see the project notes for what is planned.
